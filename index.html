Etteln as a living digital model

Real2Twin keeps a 3D model of a village up to date from ordinary photos. This page shows what exists today for Etteln, in the order we would walk you through it.

1. Your village in 3DEvery building in Etteln on a map you can tilt and click. Each one has a type, a height and a code.
2. From photos to 3DOne building, walked around with a camera, rebuilt by the computer and split into parts (walls, roof, windows). Coming soon.
3. Then and nowSlide between two surveys of the same place. This is what keeps the model true over time. Coming soon.
4. For the fire brigadeAccess roads, hydrants and assembly points on the same map. In preparation.
5. Where we arePlain-language progress: what was done, what runs now, what comes next.
Project Real2Twin, TH OWL, with Borchen-Etteln. Work in progress; data from Open.NRW and OpenStreetMap.
